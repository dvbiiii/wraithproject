Three ideas that actually help

  1. Active recall. Close the book and write what you remember. Checking afterward is where the learning sticks.
  2. Spaced repetition. Revisit a topic a day later, then a week later. Short gaps beat one long cram.
  3. One subject per loop. Switching tasks mid-session costs focus. Finish the loop, then move on.
